Legal Timekeeper

Simple, accurate time tracking for legal work. No bloat and no learning curve, just a design built for the way a transactional practice actually operates.

Most time-tracking software assumes you work on one thing at a time in neat blocks. Legal work does not fit that model: calls interrupt drafting, emails arrive mid-review, and deadlines move. The Timekeeper was built for that reality.

Say or type who the work was for, the matter, and what you did. The Timekeeper asks for anything missing, rounds the time to your billing increment, and keeps a running total for the day.

  • Speak an entry or type it, with clients and matters suggested from your past entries
  • Time recorded in tenths of an hour or quarter hours, rounded the way you bill
  • Browse any past day, then export a day, week, month, or custom period as CSV or text
  • Optional Microsoft sign-in keeps entries in sync across your devices through your own OneDrive
  • Works offline and installs to a phone home screen; no subscription required

Open the Timekeeper

Entries stay on your device and, if you sign in, in a private folder of your own OneDrive. The firm does not receive or store them.
