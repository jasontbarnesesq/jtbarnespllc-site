Legal Prompt Coach

A browser extension that coaches AI prompts for legal work as you write them, then builds them out into complete instructions before you press send.

The quality of an AI answer depends on the quality of the request. Most prompts for legal work leave out the facts a lawyer would never leave out: whose side you represent, which document and sections are in scope, what standard applies, and what the output should look like. The Prompt Coach catches those gaps while you type.

Draft in your own words in the AI tool you already use. A small score indicator sits beside the prompt box, rates the draft from 0 to 100, names the missing pieces, and with one click rebuilds the draft into a full prompt: the method for the task, your instructions, the missing parameters as fill-in fields, quality controls, and a place for your source material.

  • Works inside Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Microsoft 365 Copilot, Perplexity, and Meta AI
  • Nine profiles and more than 100 task scenarios, from drafting and document review to regulatory filings, disputes, client letters, and practice operations
  • Texas attorney advertising guardrails built into every marketing and business development prompt
  • A side panel builder for longer prompts, plus right-click coaching for text on any page
  • Scoring and building run locally in the browser; the optional Deep Coach sends a draft to Claude only when you click it, using your own API key

Request a copy

For Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. The Prompt Coach improves how a request is written; it does not review the answer, and every output still needs a lawyer’s judgment. Do not enter confidential client information into an AI tool your engagement terms do not permit.
