Jason T. Barnes leads the firm, drawing on nearly 30 years representing capital providers, investors, and those raising capital.

His work includes members of the Forbes Global 50, some of the world’s largest banks and financial institutions, private and public investment funds, and families, on transactions inside and outside the United States. He has served as a principal of registered investment advisers and, for nearly two decades, as transactional and securities counsel in the U.S. EB-5 program.
